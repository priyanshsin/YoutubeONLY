Verse 1:
Dusty roads behind me, a map creased in my hand.
Old familiar comforts, now a distant, fading land.
Sunrise paints the highway, with colours soft and deep,
A quiet hum within me, secrets I will keep.
The engine's steady rhythm, a pulse against the grey,
Carries me to futures, so many miles away.

Chorus:
Oh, city of new whispers, a silhouette so grand,
A tapestry of stories, I long to understand.
Each brick a silent promise, each shadow a soft gleam,
Am I truly ready for this vibrant, waking dream?

Verse 2:
Through bustling streets I wander, a stranger to this pace,
New faces, hurried footsteps, a smile on every face.
The coffee shop aroma, the distant church bells chime,
A different kind of cadence, defying space and time.
From a window, high above me, a melody takes flight,
Inviting me to listen, and bask within its light.

Bridge:
A little fear unwinding, a hope that starts to bloom,
No longer just observing, I step into each room.
The courage found in silence, the strength within my stride,
This new beginning beckons, nowhere left to hide.

Outro:
And as the evening settles, soft lamplight through the haze,
I feel a quiet grounding, through these unfolding days.
This city calls me closer, with every breath I take,
A brand new life is waiting, for goodness gracious sake.
