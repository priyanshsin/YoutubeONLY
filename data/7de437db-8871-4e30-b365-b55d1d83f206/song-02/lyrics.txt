Verse 1:
Raat bhar jaaga tha main, saaye ghere the,
Andhere kamron mein, ansoo bahe the.
Ek boj tha dil pe, jo utra nahi,
Har pal tha mushkil, thahra kahi.

Chorus:
Par dekho, woh aayi hai, nayi subah ki dastaan,
Dheere se khul rahe hain, band darwaaze yahan.
Ujale ki kiranein, chhu rahi hain zameen ko,
Fir se jeene ki chahat, mili hai har kahin ko.

Verse 2:
Purani yaadein, dhundli ho rahi hain,
Kal ki woh baatein, ab kahin kho rahi hain.
Hawayein jo behti hain, kuch naya laayi hain,
Toote hue khwabon ko, fir se jagayi hain.

Chorus:

Fir se jeene ki chahat, mili hai har kahin ko.

Bridge:
Har ek pal ab, lagta hai pyara,
Is nayi roshni ka, mila hai sahara.
Himmat ab aayi hai, badhne ki raahon par,
Chhod chale hain hum, saare dukhon ko pichhe ab.
